### Hey there, I'm Marco!

I'm an **AI Engineer** based in Amsterdam, Netherlands, with a deep passion for designing and deploying systems that are both intelligent and trustworthy. My focus is on bridging the gap between cutting-edge models and production-ready applications, making them scalable, secure, and genuinely helpful.

My core specializations lie in **Agentic Workflows**, **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, and most critically, **LLM Safety**. I'm a firm believer that the smartest systems are the ones you can rely on.

---

### My Focus

I come from a strong **Full-Stack and Cloud-Native** foundation, which gives me a holistic view of the development lifecycle. This means I'm not just focused on the model, but on the entire end-to-end system, from the front-end to robust MLOps pipelines.

I love working with tools and concepts like:

* **GenAI Applications & Agentic Workflows:** I enjoy building complex, multi-agent systems, using frameworks like Pydantic-AI, that interact in real-time to solve intricate problems, such as my work on the CareerKit intelligent CV customizer. I have expertise in GenAI applications and Agentic Workflows.

* **LLM Safety & Governance:** This is paramount. I have hands-on experience in implementing rigorous safety guardrails, ensuring that the model can only do less harm than good. I ensure strict compliance with protocols like **GDPR and HIPAA** through methods like conversation anonymization and encryption.

* **MLOps and Production Readiness:** I leverage **CI/CD** and tools like **GitHub Actions** and **Docker** to ensure continuous deployment and reliable releases. I've established real-time production evaluation systems, using techniques like an LLM-as-User for realistic conversation testing and an LLMJudge for tracking core metrics.

* **Cloud Architecture:** My applications are designed to be deployed securely and scalably, primarily leveraging **AWS** services like EC2, Lambda, and API Gateway for serverless architectures.
